Garage Floor Coating details

Why Do Woodstock Garage Floors Need Coating?

Vehicles here pull in loaded with slush and road brine from the daily run on Highway 401, and that brine sits directly on bare concrete until it dries or gets tracked back out again.

Left alone, that salt load speeds up the freeze-thaw damage the floor is already fighting through winter. A coating removes the problem at the source by sealing the surface so brine never reaches the concrete underneath it.

What Is The Best Garage Flooring System For This Climate?

For a freeze-thaw climate like this one, the combination that holds up best pairs three layers: an epoxy base coat, a flake broadcast, and a polyaspartic finish on top. The base layer keys into the slab and builds real thickness underneath everything else. The flakes hide dirt and add texture. The top coat closes it out with abrasion resistance, a faster cure in cool weather, and colour that will not shift under UV light the way plain epoxy can.

How Long Does A Coated Garage Floor Last?

Most garage floor coatings installed this way run 15 to 20 years before needing real attention. What determines that number is less about the finish coat and more about whether the slab was properly ground before anything else went down.

Can Cracked Or Pitted Concrete Be Coated?

Usually, yes. Cracks are routed and filled, and pitted or spalled patches are built back up level before the base coat goes on. Where a slab shows real structural movement or serious heaving, we say so at the quote rather than coating over damage that will only resurface later.

How Long Before I Can Park On It?

Foot traffic is generally fine around 12 hours after the final coat, with vehicles clear to return in 24 to 48 hours depending on the system and the temperature during cure. A polyaspartic top coat is part of why that window is as short as it is.

What Does Epoxy Flooring For A Garage Cost?

The size of the garage, the shape the slab is in, and the system chosen all move the price. A standard two-car garage in reasonable condition sits in a predictable range; heavy crack repair or stripping old paint adds time and shifts the number upward. We measure in person and confirm a fixed figure in writing before booking anything.
