Flake Epoxy Flooring details

What Is Flake Epoxy Flooring?

A flake system starts with a pigmented epoxy base coat, into which coloured vinyl chips are broadcast while the resin is still wet. A clear polyaspartic top coat then locks the chips in place and seals the whole surface.

Beyond the look, those chips do real work. They break the surface up visually, which hides tire marks, dust and light scuffing far better than a single flat colour ever could between cleanings.

Why Is Flake Flooring Good For Woodstock Garages?

Garages here spend a good part of the year dealing with road brine off Highway 401 and the freeze-thaw swing that comes with it. A flake floor's texture, paired with a slip-resistant additive, holds footing on a wet surface noticeably better than a smooth solid-colour finish.

The multi-tone blends also mean a small stain or a dropped socket does not stand out the way it would against plain grey, so the floor keeps looking finished between deep cleans.

What Colours Are Available?

Blends range from a single restrained grey to combinations that read almost like natural stone once the chips are down and sealed. A sample board comes along to the estimate itself, so the colour decision happens under your own garage lighting instead of off a screen.

How Slip Resistant Is A Flake Floor?

The broadcast chips add texture on their own, and we can work a fine slip-resistant additive into the top coat for garages that regularly see snow melt tracked in. Together they hold footing even when the floor is wet from tires or boots.

How Durable Is Flake Epoxy Flooring?

A properly installed flake system shrugs off daily vehicle traffic, jack stands, dropped tools and hot tire pickup without chipping. The polyaspartic top coat resists abrasion, so the chip layer underneath stays protected across the life of the floor instead of wearing thin along the tire paths first.

How Is Flake Epoxy Flooring Maintained?

There is nothing to reapply and nothing to seal again later. Run a broom or a dust mop over it on a normal cleaning schedule, and reach for a damp mop and mild cleaner whenever it needs more than that. Once winter driving picks up, hose or wipe off the brine that drips from the vehicle instead of leaving it to sit for weeks at a time.
