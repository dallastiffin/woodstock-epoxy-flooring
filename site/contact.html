Contact details and what to expect

Request A Free Estimate

There is no charge for an estimate, and the number you get back is put in writing. Have the rough size of the space ready, along with what the room is used for and how the concrete currently looks.

If the slab has cracking, staining or old paint on it, a couple of photos ahead of time help us plan the visit. From there we set a time to come look at the floor in person and check the concrete before anything is finalized.

The figure you receive covers the system, the colour choice, and roughly how long the job will take, and it does not move once work begins.

Service Areas

Our crews work throughout Woodstock and the rest of Oxford County, including Ingersoll, Tillsonburg, Norwich, Tavistock, Beachville, Innerkip and Thamesford.

Larger commercial projects outside that radius are also worth a call; ask when you reach out.

What To Expect

Once you get in touch, we set up a visit at a time that suits you. That visit covers a look at the slab for cracking, moisture and buildup, along with a walk-through of colour and finish options.

A written number usually follows within a day or two of that visit.

From there, we pick an install date. A garage or basement is typically finished within one to two days once grinding starts; larger commercial spaces get scheduled around your hours wherever we can manage it.

Once the floor is finished, you get written care instructions along with how long to wait before putting it back into daily use.
